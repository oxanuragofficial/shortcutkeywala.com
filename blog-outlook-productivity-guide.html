📧 Outlook Productivity Guide: 42 Shortcuts to Master Your Inbox

January 10, 2024 10 min read 42 shortcuts Email Productivity

Microsoft Outlook is the most popular email client for business professionals. Learning Outlook keyboard shortcuts can help you manage your inbox faster, schedule meetings efficiently, and stay on top of your tasks. The average professional spends 28% of their workweek on email - shortcuts can cut that time in half!

Pro Tip: Start with the most frequently used shortcuts like Ctrl+R (Reply) and Ctrl+Shift+M (New Message). Master these first, then expand to calendar and task shortcuts.

Top 10 Outlook Shortcuts You'll Use Every Day:
  1. Ctrl + R - Reply (fastest way to respond)
  2. Ctrl + Shift + M - New Message (compose from anywhere)
  3. Ctrl + Enter - Send (send without reaching for mouse)
  4. Ctrl + 1 / 2 / 3 - Switch between Mail, Calendar, Contacts
  5. Delete - Archive/Delete email
  6. Ctrl + Q / Ctrl + U - Mark as Read/Unread
  7. Ctrl + Shift + A - New Appointment (quick scheduling)
  8. Ctrl + E - Search (find anything instantly)
  9. Ctrl + F - Forward email
  10. Insert - Flag for follow-up
Inbox Zero Strategy with Shortcuts:
  • Use Ctrl + R to reply immediately to quick emails
  • Use Ctrl + Shift + M to draft new messages
  • Use Insert to flag emails that need follow-up
  • Use Delete to archive emails you don't need
  • Use Ctrl + Q to mark newsletters as read quickly
